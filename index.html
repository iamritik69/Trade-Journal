Trade Analysis — Score, Duration, Filters

Score rule-based hai: 50 base + R × 10, risk 0.5% ya kam ho to +10, Out of Zone ho to −10, 0–100 ke beech. Ye tumhare trade ki quality ka andaza hai, koi official score nahi hai.

Leaderboard — Top Trades (apni hi trades me se)

Ye single-user site hai, isliye asli dusre traders ki ranking yahan nahi hai, aur koi fake community ranking nahi banayi gayi hai. Neeche tumhare apne best trades ki ranking hai, Net R ke hisaab se.

AI News Analysis — Gold aur Pairs ke liye

Important: Ye analysis rule-based hai, koi real AI model ya price prediction nahi hai.
